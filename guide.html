Dungeon LootR First hours

Beginner Guide

What this game is

Dungeon LootR is a Hack & Slash Dungeon ARPG. You run randomly generated dungeons, play 30+ classes, fight to the boss, and extract with your loot intact.

  • Spin for classes
  • Enter dungeons for loot
  • Experiment with stat builds
  • Play Boss Rush or Challenge Dungeon for bigger challenges

Max player level is 200. Premium Plus players earn +5% Player EXP and +10% Class EXP.

Using bugs or exploits is prohibited and can get you actioned.

Official Discord: discord.com/invite/dungeonlootr. Play: Roblox Dungeon LootR. Group: ClickBytes.

First hours

Talk to the Guide NPC. That is the short onboarding tutorial that gets you moving. The tutorial kit is Ronin.

The Group Reward NPC pays out rewards for joining the group. The lobby also has a Buy/Sell merchant — do not spend early Coins on every refresh armor; keep Coins for Forge.

Hour one is this loop — spin → run → extract → forge (only if you got good loot) — not a tour of every lobby button:

  • Open Classes and spin or equip a class (Normal Spin / Lucky Spin).
  • The first dungeon is Bandit's Den. The next map is Goblin's Stronghold. You do not jump to Hard: each map unlocks Easy → Normal → Hard → Nightmare or the next map.
  • Clear rooms, grab chests, push the floor boss, then Extract with loot intact.
  • Spend ores at the Forge on the piece you actually clear with. The Training Area next to Forge is for checking your damage and class feel (equipment can change damage, cooldown reduction, speed ...).
  • Claim Daily / Weekly quests. And Codes, but they are optional (Menu → More → Codes).

Bank one clean extract before you force Hard / Nightmare just because the lobby button exists. Deeper zones raise loot rarity and death risk. Do not plan hour one around titles (God Hunter, Devil Hunter, and the rest).

Combat

In dungeons or raids, enemies will hit you. Some have specials, some don’t. Your answers are abilities, M1s, parry, and dodges.
To kill, spam abilities. A lot of them grant i-frames, a dodge, or a parry window — spend that on the hits that matter.
To live through big hordes or heavy bosses, dash (Q on PC). You get i-frames and you leave the attack’s range.
M1s are filler: finish a low-HP target, weave between abilities, or proc a passive. Don’t M1 over a ready skill unless that’s the plan.
Parry is for heavy hits or a stack of hits at once. Smash F on PC for a moment to make a parry, smash it like an M1.

You also have a block bar. It does not carry fights — dodge or parry instead.

Some enemies wear a light blue shield bar. Not every enemy has one. Break that bar before normal damage applies. Some enemy types can put that shield on themselves and on allies.

Archers could not be the first target, but they are the pack that chips and annoys the most if you leave them up.

Practice parry on trash telegraphs before you take that timing into Boss Rush. M1s fill the gap while skills cool down; the four skills are the primary clear tools. Only a couple of classes have an Ultimate, recharged by parries, dodges, and kills.

Cauldrons refill potions. They show up every few rooms, not once per run.

Rejuvenation Tonic restores 40% of your Max HP.

Classes

Classes have rarities: Rare, Epic, Legendary, Mythic, Celestial, Exotic. Full kits live on Classes. The current competitive snapshot is the Tier List — click a name there to open its page.

Most starter-pool classes list obtainment as Class Summon. Use a Normal Spin or a Lucky Spin in the Summoning menu. Lucky Spin raises Celestial and Exotic odds and slightly lowers Rare, Epic, and Legendary. Some classes instead come from Exotic Summoning, Battlepass, event shops, dungeon drops, Boss Rush, or NPC quests.

Class XP Essence grants 100 Class XP to your active class instantly. Class sheets also list masteries (shown LOCKED until earned) and, on some kits, a mastery passive or an Evolve tab.

Aspect Gem rolls a random Aspect onto your currently equipped class. Those drop from Challenge Dungeon milestone rewards — not a first-hour item.

Stats

Open Stats. The game marks one or more stats as Recommended for your current class. Always dump SP into Recommended first — before Vitality, before off-axis damage, before an even spread.

  • Strength — Raises Attack and Skill Damage for Physical classes, plus Skill Crit Damage for all classes.
  • Dexterity — Raises Attack and Skill Damage for Ranged classes, plus Crit Rate, Attack Speed, and Dodge Rate.
  • Intelligence — Raises Attack and Skill Damage for Magic classes, plus Cooldown Reduction and Skill Crit Rate for all classes.
  • Vitality — Raises Max HP and grants a small amount of HP Regen.

Spent SP can be re-spec'd — reset and moved between Strength, Dexterity, Intelligence, and Vitality. After a re-spec, fill Recommended again first.

Late titles (God Hunter, Devil Hunter, and the rest) add mixed stats that include Luck. Do not plan hour one around farming them. More copy: Extras → Stats.

Dungeons

Dungeons are the core maps. Clear rooms, grab chests, push the floor boss, and hit blessing pads for short-term buffs (stats / XP / Coins / cooldown reduction). Difficulties: Easy, Normal, Hard, Nightmare, Endless. You unlock them in that order on a map, then the next map. Clears pay XP, gear, and coins.

Extract is the win condition. If you die before extract, you lose that run’s bag. Beginners should bank one clean extract before forcing Hard / Nightmare.

Some rooms have a door or opening into a mini side room. That pocket can hold chests, or nothing. Some of those doors ask for a key; some do not.

Maps

  • Bandit's Den
  • Goblin's Stronghold
  • Forgotten Ruins
  • The Catacombs

  • Nightmare+

    Awakened Devil — rare extract: Devil Hearts, turned in at Valen and Jetstream.

  • Nightmare+

    Scarlet Knight — special boss. Guaranteed Exotic Ore. Rare drop: Exotic Shattered Armor.

  • City of Mages

When a side room does ask for a key: Bronze (Tier I), Silver (Tier 2), Gold (Tier 3), Platinum (Tier 4), Celestial (Tier 5). Save higher keys for those gated rooms instead of spending them the moment you see a door. See Index → Items → Keys.

Gear Score is only a rough read of how strong a piece looks. It is not directly proportional to damage, DPS, or DPM, and Nightmare does not gate on Gear Score.

Forge

The Forge Guide NPC breaks down the Forge. Extract first — the Forge cannot save loot you died with. The bench crafts items and upgrades equipment in a couple of ways. Ores refine into Ingots; Ingots upgrade equipment of the matching rarity. Exotic Ingots only come from Exotic Ore at that bench.

The Training Area sits beside the Forge. Use it to check your damage and how the class feels with your current equipment (damage, cooldown reduction, speed, and similar). Upgrade the weapon or armor you actually clear with. Craft ingots when you are short for that path. Reforge (affixes / stats) after the piece is worth keeping. Do not dump Protection Scrolls on filler — those often come from Challenge later.

  • Protection Scroll — stops a forge from downgrading on failure. Challenge Dungeon rewards/chests, or the shop.
  • Purity Stone — raises a forge attempt’s success chance. Consumed on success or fail. Challenge Dungeon, Boss Rush, or crafted at the Forge.
  • Affix Seal — preserve an affix type and value during Reforge. 1 seal per locked affix per roll. Lock up to 3; leave at least 1 unlocked. Does not protect quality rerolls.
  • Reforge Stone — used at the Forge to re-roll an item’s stats. Drops from Mythic+ locked-room chests, Boss Rush bosses by tier, Challenge Dungeon milestones, and the Battlepass.

Loot luck

Luck potions grant a temporary Loot Luck boost and stack with other Luck tiers. Eligible drops only. Duration on the potion cards is 10 minutes.

  • Luck Potion I — +5% rare drop chance and improved equipment rarity. Battlepass.
  • Luck Potion II — +10%. Battlepass and Challenge Dungeon.
  • Luck Potion III — +15%. Battlepass and Challenge Dungeon.

Quests, Battlepass, AFK, codes

Daily and Weekly quests are the free progress layer: Coins, Stars, Normal Spins, and Lucky Spins. Clear them on the class you already run — do not spin a throwaway kit just to tick a name. Claim those spins before you spend Coins (or Robux) on extra spins.

  • Daily — fast Coins / Stars / spins. Knock them out before long AFK.
  • Weekly — bigger spin rewards. One focused session.
  • Limited — event windows. Grab while live.
  • Battle Pass — claim idle nodes on the free track. Demonbane is a Level 50 Battlepass Reward (Free Track). The Great Mage NPC also tells you that (tier 50).

The AFK NPC sends you to the AFK world. Without Premium or passes, rewards tick every 20 minutes. The server force-rejoins you every 16 minutes so you are not kicked. Park AFK after you have banked today’s dungeon quest, not instead of a clean extract + Forge cycle.

Codes: Menu → More → Codes. Some strings need the ClickBytes group. Codes rotate — use the official Discord for the current list. Ignore aggregator “ALL WORKING” posts. Details: Extras.

Other modes — when you can enter

Endless is a chained dungeon loop: same rooms, over and over, for gold, XP, gear, and loot. It does not play different from a normal dungeon. The length is the change.

Challenges — level 55+ to queue. Climb floors until you die or you hit the end. The run pays out when you leave. Milestones every 10 floors drop extra gifts. Rewards can include evolution materials and class-requirement items, plus coins and a mix of everything else. Multiple Challenges rotate. Info: Challenges and the Challenge Guide NPC.

Boss Rush / Raids — level 67+ to walk in. You fight a stacked boss across floors that get stronger as you climb. People run it for new classes, the occasional cosmetic, and some equipment. Most of the real payout is locked behind floor 100, the cap. Boss Rush Skip Tickets skip ahead in 10-floor steps (up to Floor 50) at the select screen; skipping forfeits leaderboard time for the whole party, Floor Milestone rewards are still earned. Info: Raids.

Floor 100 rare drops / crafts include class items for Anti Magic, Honored One, and Cursed King (50 matching fragments to craft). Great Mage Staff (Demonbane) is Battlepass Tier 50, or a rare drop from the Great Mage at Boss Rush Floor 100.

Events

Events are a rotating playlist. Three events are covered here. Full boards: Events.

Payload — island-hopping waves. Lobby is level 40+; each difficulty has its own gate (Easy 40+, Normal 75+, Hard 100+, Nightmare 150+). Islands throw two enemy waves; chests and short-term buffs between islands. NPCs can fight alongside you. Last island: boss Torus. Currency: Aether Marks, spent at the Payload Shop on the lobby boat (classes, titles, gear, and more). Daily shop stock resets at midnight UTC. Fae is bought from that shop.

Magic Unleashed — high-end raid boss, a hard clear, not a farm loop. Clears pay event cosmetics, event equipment, Mage Coins, and mixed drops. Spend Mage Coins in the event shop. Spell Breaker and Cryomancer list that shop; Dark Professor lists the raid on Impossible Difficulty.

Tournament — timed damage race. Solo and Duo. Record the full run; staff will not lock podium or pay without footage. Modifiers rotate every two weeks. Extra rules are on the Events page (review window, no exploits, no alt lockout bypass, lifetime moderation-flag total over 100 forfeits that season).

Later — class evolutions and titles

Not first-hour goals. Confirmed Evolve paths on Classes:

  • Azure Devil → Awakened Devil EX via Valen (Mastery 50 + 1 Devil Heart + 500,000 coins, awards Judgements Edge). Same class also evolves to Jetstream via the Jetstream NPC (Mastery 50 + 3 Devil Hearts + Exotic Shattered Armor + 200,000 coins, awards Cybernetic Katana). Devil Hearts: rare extract from the Awakened Devil on Frostspire (Nightmare+).
  • Honored One → Unrestricted via Unrestricted Fighter (Heavenly Fragments; Mastery 25 + 1,000,000 coins; awards Inverted Spear). Heavenly Fragments: 15% chance per player from Challenge Dungeon boss waves past Wave 40 (the bosses on waves 50, 60, 70, and on).

Title NPCs (Mastery 50 plus coin, and sometimes extra mats) include Artemis (God Hunter), Cursed King (King of Curses, 1M coins), Forge Archon (Infinite Blade Works, 1M coins), Great Mage (Demonbane title, 1M coins), Devil Hunter (Sinister Triggers Mastery 50, 2M coins, 5 Devil Hunter, 2 Exotic Ingots), and Kage (Black Falcon, 1M coins). Those titles add mixed stats, including Luck.

Habits that wipe early loot

  • Dying with an unextracted bag — extract on a clean boss kill; greed another zone only if you can still live.
  • Spreading SP evenly — fill the Recommended stats first.
  • Skipping Daily / Weekly — those spins are the free layer next to AFK and Battlepass.
  • Trusting expired “ALL WORKING” code lists — Menu → More → Codes, and the official Discord.
  • Skipping parry practice on trash, then taking that timing into Boss Rush.
  • Spending Protection Scrolls and Forge mats on filler instead of the piece you clear with.
  • The lobby Buy/Sell merchant — spending early Coins on every refresh armor delays Forge.
  • Sitting on block instead of dodge or parry.
