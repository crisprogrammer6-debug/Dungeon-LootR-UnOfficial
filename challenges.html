Challenges

Info

Challenges sit next to Endless: you clear floors, hit milestones, and those floors can pay out very strong rewards depending on how deep you are. Along the way you also pull evolution materials, new classes requirements, coins, and a mix of everything else.

You need level 55+ to queue. There are multiple Challenges — they rotate below.

Rewards

Depending on the Challenge, you get different rewards and/or class evolution materials.
