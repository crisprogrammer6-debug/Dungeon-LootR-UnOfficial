Gamemodes

Dungeons

Info

Dungeons are the core maps you run to progress. Clear rooms packed with different enemies, grab chests, and push to the floor boss. Along the way you can hit altars for short-term buffs.

This mode has multiple difficulties. On each map you unlock them in order — Easy, then Normal, then Hard, then Nightmare — before the next map opens:

  • Easy
  • Normal
  • Hard
  • Nightmare
  • Endless

Clears pay out XP, gear, and coins — enough to keep climbing.

Challenges

Info

Challenges are a dungeon variant: you climb floors as far as you can until you die, or you hit the end. You need level 55 to queue.

The run pays out when you leave. It has some milestones each 10 floors that drop extra gifts and rewards.

Boss Rush

Info

Boss Rush is a hard dungeon set. You fight a stacked boss across multiple difficulties.

The objective is new classes, a few farm routes, and not much else.

Endless

Info

Endless is a chained dungeon loop — same rooms, over and over. You run it for gold, XP, gear, and general loot.

It doesn’t play different from a normal dungeon. The only real change is the length: it just keeps going.
